# Privacy Policy for ComfierUI

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

ComfierUI is designed to function as a direct client for connecting to a ComfyUI server selected and controlled by the user.

The ComfierUI application itself does not operate a developer-controlled system for collecting, storing, selling, or profiling personal user information.

ComfierUI may, however, include third-party services such as Google AdMob that independently collect and process certain information as described in this Privacy Policy.

## Information Collected by ComfierUI

ComfierUI does not require a ComfierUI account and does not operate a developer-controlled analytics or user-data collection backend.

The developer does not collect information such as:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Physical addresses
- Account credentials
- Contact lists
- Photos or media for developer analytics purposes
- Precise location data
- Usage analytics
- Developer-operated crash analytics
- Workflow contents
- Generated images
- Model information
- ComfyUI server contents

ComfierUI does not send workflow activity, generated media, model information, or ComfyUI server contents to developer-controlled servers.

Third-party services included in or used alongside ComfierUI may independently collect certain device, advertising, network, diagnostic, or interaction information as described below.

## Connections to ComfyUI Servers

ComfierUI connects directly to a ComfyUI server address entered or selected by the user.

This may include:

- A local network address
- A private VPN address
- A Tailscale address
- Another user-configured ComfyUI host

Data exchanged with a ComfyUI server is sent directly between the Android device and the server selected by the user.

ComfierUI does not route ComfyUI traffic through developer-controlled servers.

Users are responsible for the privacy and security of the ComfyUI server or network they choose to connect to.

## Locally Stored Information

ComfierUI may store application settings locally on the Android device in order to provide normal app functionality.

This may include settings such as:

- Saved connection profiles
- Host addresses
- UI preferences
- Display settings
- Performance settings
- Connector offset settings
- Other user-selected application preferences

This information remains on the device unless it is otherwise handled by the Android operating system, device backup features, or services enabled by the user.

The developer does not receive this locally stored information.

## ComfierUI Companion

ComfierUI may optionally interact with the open-source ComfierUI Companion extension installed on the user's ComfyUI host.

Companion functionality may include:

- Detecting the installed Companion version
- Requesting supported host-side actions
- Initiating supported model downloads directly on the ComfyUI host
- Receiving download status or progress information

Large model files downloaded through ComfierUI Companion are downloaded directly by the host computer and are not routed through or stored on the Android device by ComfierUI.

Use of ComfierUI Companion is optional.

## Advertising

ComfierUI uses **Google AdMob** to display an app-open advertisement.

ComfierUI does not operate its own advertising profile or collect personal information on developer-controlled servers for advertising purposes.

Google AdMob, as a third-party advertising service, may collect and process information necessary to serve, measure, secure, personalize where permitted, and improve advertisements.

Depending on the user's device, region, consent choices, Google settings, and applicable law, this information may include:

- IP address
- Advertising identifiers or other device identifiers
- Device and operating system information
- Approximate location derived from network information
- Ad impressions
- Ad interaction data
- Diagnostic and performance information
- App-related information used for advertising or measurement
- Information used for fraud prevention
- Information used for frequency capping
- Information used for advertising measurement
- Information used for ad personalization where permitted

Information collected and processed by Google AdMob is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policies and terms.

## Personalized, Non-Personalized, and Limited Ads

Depending on applicable law, user region, device settings, consent choices, and Google AdMob configuration, ComfierUI may display personalized, non-personalized, or limited advertisements.

Personalized advertisements may use information such as previous activity, interests, demographic information, device information, or location information to influence ad selection where permitted.

Non-personalized advertisements are not selected based on a user's previous behavior. However, Google may still process information such as IP address, coarse or approximate location, device identifiers, and other technical information for purposes including:

- Ad delivery
- Frequency capping
- Aggregated reporting
- Measurement
- Security
- Fraud prevention

Limited ads may use a more restricted set of data depending on applicable privacy requirements and consent status.

Where legally required, users may be presented with privacy or consent choices before advertising data is used for purposes requiring consent.

ComfierUI does not independently create advertising profiles, combine advertising information with ComfyUI workflow activity, or receive personal advertising profiles created by Google.

## Third-Party Services

ComfierUI may rely on or interact with third-party services selected, installed, or enabled by the user or included as part of application functionality.

Examples may include:

- Google AdMob
- Tailscale
- ComfyUI
- ComfyUI-Manager
- ComfierUI Companion
- Android operating system services
- User-selected network or VPN providers

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

ComfierUI is not responsible for data collection or processing performed independently by third-party services.

Users should review the privacy practices of third-party services they choose to use.

## Network Security

ComfyUI does not provide authentication by default.

Users should avoid exposing a ComfyUI server directly to the public internet unless they have properly secured the environment.

For remote access, a trusted private network or VPN such as Tailscale is recommended.

ComfierUI does not provide additional authentication or security for an otherwise publicly exposed ComfyUI server.

## Children's Privacy

ComfierUI does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults through developer-controlled servers.

Because ComfierUI itself does not operate a user-account or user-profiling system, the developer does not maintain profiles of users based on age.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob may apply their own age-related policies, consent requirements, and advertising restrictions.

## Data Sharing

ComfierUI does not sell personal user information.

ComfierUI does not provide workflow contents, generated images, model information, saved server addresses, or other locally stored ComfierUI data to advertisers.

Google AdMob and other third-party services may independently collect, process, or share information according to their own privacy policies, terms, advertising configurations, and applicable law.

Any information collected directly by Google AdMob is governed by Google's privacy practices.

## Data Retention

Because ComfierUI does not collect personal user information on developer-controlled servers, the developer does not maintain or retain personal user profiles.

Locally stored application preferences remain on the user's device until they are:

- Changed by the user
- Cleared through Android settings
- Removed by uninstalling the application
- Otherwise handled by Android or device backup systems

Third-party services such as Google AdMob may retain information according to their own retention policies.

- Consent interfaces displayed where required by applicable law

The availability and behavior of these controls may vary by device, region, Google account configuration, and applicable law.
