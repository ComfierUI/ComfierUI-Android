# Privacy Policy for ComfierUI

**Last updated: September 10, 2026**

ComfierUI is designed to function as a direct client for connecting to a ComfyUI server selected and controlled by the user.

ComfierUI itself does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal user information to the developer.

## Information Collected by ComfierUI

ComfierUI does not collect personal information.

The application does not require a ComfierUI account and does not collect information such as:

- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Physical addresses
- Account credentials
- Contact lists
- Photos or media for developer analytics purposes
- Location data
- Advertising identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Crash analytics
- Device identifiers
- Workflow contents
- Generated images
- Model information
- ComfyUI server contents

ComfierUI does not operate a developer-controlled analytics backend for collecting user activity.

## Connections to ComfyUI Servers

ComfierUI connects directly to a ComfyUI server address entered or selected by the user.

This may include:

- A local network address
- A private VPN address
- A Tailscale address
- Another user-configured ComfyUI host

Data exchanged with a ComfyUI server is sent directly between the Android device and the server selected by the user.

ComfierUI does not route ComfyUI traffic through developer-controlled servers.

Users are responsible for the privacy and security of the ComfyUI server or network they choose to connect to.

## Locally Stored Information

ComfierUI may store application settings locally on the Android device in order to provide normal app functionality.

This may include settings such as:

- Saved connection profiles
- Host addresses
- UI preferences
- Display settings
- Performance settings
- Connector offset settings
- Other user-selected application preferences

This information remains on the device unless it is otherwise handled by the Android operating system, device backup features, or services enabled by the user.

The developer does not receive this locally stored information.

## ComfierUI Companion

ComfierUI may optionally interact with the open-source ComfierUI Companion extension installed on the user's ComfyUI host.

Companion functionality may include:

- Detecting the installed Companion version
- Requesting supported host-side actions
- Initiating supported model downloads directly on the ComfyUI host
- Receiving download status or progress information

Large model files downloaded through ComfierUI Companion are downloaded directly by the host computer and are not routed through or stored on the Android device by ComfierUI.

Use of ComfierUI Companion is optional.

## Advertising

ComfierUI may display an app-open advertisement using **Google AdMob**.

ComfierUI itself does not collect user information for advertising purposes.

However, Google AdMob may independently collect and process information in order to provide, measure, personalize, prevent fraud in, or otherwise operate advertising services.

Depending on the user's device, location, consent choices, and Google settings, this information may include:

- Advertising identifiers
- Device information
- IP address
- Approximate location derived from network information
- Ad interaction data
- Diagnostic information
- App-related information used for advertising or measurement
- Other information described in Google's advertising and privacy documentation

Information collected by Google AdMob is processed by Google under Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Users can learn more about Google's privacy practices at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information about Google advertising technologies is available at:

https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## Personalized and Non-Personalized Ads

Depending on applicable law, user region, device settings, consent status, and Google AdMob configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Where required, Google or its consent systems may provide users with privacy or consent choices related to advertising.

ComfierUI does not independently create advertising profiles or combine advertising data with ComfyUI usage information.

## Third-Party Services

ComfierUI may rely on third-party services selected or enabled by the user.

Examples may include:

- Google AdMob
- Tailscale
- ComfyUI
- ComfyUI-Manager
- ComfierUI Companion
- Android operating system services
- User-selected network or VPN providers

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

ComfierUI is not responsible for data collection or processing performed independently by third-party services.

## Network Security

ComfyUI does not provide authentication by default.

Users should avoid exposing a ComfyUI server directly to the public internet unless they have properly secured the environment.

For remote access, a trusted private network or VPN such as Tailscale is recommended.

## Children's Privacy

ComfierUI does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Because ComfierUI itself does not operate a user-data collection system, the developer does not maintain profiles of users based on age.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob may apply their own age-related policies and requirements.

## Data Sharing

ComfierUI does not sell user information.

ComfierUI does not share user information with advertisers because ComfierUI itself does not collect user information.

Any data collected directly by Google AdMob or another third-party service is governed by that third party's privacy practices.

## Data Retention

Because ComfierUI does not collect user information on developer-controlled servers, the developer does not retain personal user data.

Locally stored application preferences remain on the user's device until they are changed, cleared, removed by uninstalling the app, or otherwise handled by Android or device backup systems.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own retention policies.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ComfierUI features, advertising behavior, third-party integrations, legal requirements, or data practices change.

The current version of this Privacy Policy will be published in the official ComfierUI Android repository.
